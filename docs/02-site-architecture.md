# 02 — Site Architecture

## Recommended routes

```txt
/
├── /work
│   └── /work/[slug]
├── /services
├── /art
│   └── /art/[slug]
├── /about
├── /journal
│   └── /journal/[slug]
├── /inquire
└── /privacy
```

Optional later:
- `/prints`
- `/events`
- `/press`
- `/community`

## Navigation

Desktop:

`JOURNEY STUDIO | WORK | SERVICES | ART | ABOUT | JOURNAL | CONTACT | START A PROJECT`

Mobile:
- logo
- menu toggle
- full-screen dark menu
- primary CTA pinned near bottom

## Homepage flow

1. Hero
2. Approach / manifesto
3. Services
4. Selected work
5. Who we work with
6. Art / prints
7. Community / cultural work
8. Journal
9. Project inquiry
10. Footer

## Work

Grid of selected projects.

Filters may include:
- Photography
- Film
- Brand Content
- Creative Direction
- Events
- Community
- Art

Do not overcomplicate filters in v1.

## Case study

Suggested structure:

1. Hero image/video
2. Project metadata
3. Challenge / context
4. Creative idea
5. Visual gallery
6. Maheen's role
7. Deliverables
8. Results, only when verified
9. Related work
10. Start a Project CTA

## Art

Purpose:
Show Maheen as an artist, not just a service provider.

Sections:
- artist statement
- selected artworks
- exhibitions
- curation
- digital / 3D / NFT work
- prints
- commissions
- collector inquiry

## About

Cover:
- multidisciplinary creative practice
- photography
- film / content
- art
- creative strategy
- community
- selected qualifications
- exhibitions / talks / curation
- personal perspective

Do not turn About into a résumé dump.

## Journal

Use for:
- creative process
- event reflections
- photography stories
- behind the scenes
- creative life
- art
- travel
- technology / culture
