# 04 — Content and Voice

## Voice principles

Journey Studio copy should feel:

- clear
- observant
- warm
- creative
- slightly poetic
- visually aware
- concise

It should not sound like a large ad agency.

## Preferred language

Use:

- stories
- visual worlds
- cinematic
- connection
- culture
- people
- places
- community
- thoughtful
- creative
- perspective
- experience
- collaboration
- meaningful
- human

## Avoid

- disruptive
- game-changing
- world-class
- growth hacking
- innovative solutions
- one-stop shop
- best-in-class
- 360-degree
- synergy
- cutting-edge, unless context truly calls for it

## Sample homepage copy

### Hero

A creative studio for brands, people and communities who believe in meaningful stories.

### Approach

We make work that people can feel. From a photograph to a campaign to a room full of people, every project begins with a point of view and ends with connection.

### Services intro

Different formats. One visual language.

### Work intro

Selected stories across people, places, brands and culture.

### Art intro

Art that begins in a moment and keeps living after it.

### Inquiry

Tell us what you're building.

## Calls to action

Preferred:
- Start a Project
- View Work
- Explore Art
- See the Story
- Work Together
- Read More
- See Case Study

Avoid:
- Learn More everywhere
- Get Started
- Book a Discovery Call as the dominant site language
