# 11 — SEO and Accessibility

## Metadata

Homepage title example:

`Journey Studio — Cinematic Content, Photography & Creative Direction`

Description:

`Journey Studio creates cinematic photography, film, brand content, creative direction and cultural experiences from Toronto to wherever the story leads.`

Adjust after final positioning is approved.

## Open Graph

Create:
- branded 1200 × 630 OG image
- Journey Studio wordmark
- strong cinematic image
- subtle blue accent

## Structured data

Use:
- `Organization` or `ProfessionalService` where appropriate
- `Person` for Maheen on About
- `CreativeWork` or `VisualArtwork` where useful for art
- `Article` for journal posts

Do not add fake ratings.

## Accessibility

Required:
- semantic headings
- one `h1` per page
- visible focus styles
- keyboard navigation
- alt text
- reduced motion
- proper form labels
- useful error announcements
- no text baked into imagery where important
- sufficient contrast
- mobile menu focus management

## Image alt style

Describe what matters visually.

Bad:
`image`

Better:
`Silhouetted figure standing in blue light against a Toronto night skyline.`

Do not keyword-stuff alt text.
