# 00 — Master Build Brief

## Brand

**Journey Studio**

### Core idea

A creative studio for brands, people, and communities who believe in meaningful stories.

Journey Studio lives at the intersection of:

- content
- photography
- film
- creative direction
- brand storytelling
- community
- events
- art
- technology
- wellness
- culture

The work should feel human before it feels commercial.

## Main business objective

The website must help Maheen get more:

- freelance photography work
- filmmaking / videography projects
- content-creation work
- creative-direction work
- social-content projects
- community and event work
- creative-strategy projects
- brand collaborations
- UGC / creator campaigns
- art commissions
- print sales
- speaking / cultural opportunities

## Audience

Primary:

- brands
- creative agencies
- founders
- marketing teams
- event organizers
- cultural organizations
- technology companies
- wellness brands
- Web3 / emerging-tech communities
- art collectors

Secondary:

- other creators
- collaborators
- curators
- journalists
- community organizers

## First impression

Within 5 seconds, a new visitor should understand:

1. Journey Studio creates cinematic visual stories.
2. The work spans content, brands, people, communities, and art.
3. The creator behind it has a distinct visual point of view.
4. The visitor can explore work or start a project.

## Tone

Concise.
Human.
Visual.
Confident.
Curious.
Not corporate.

## Site philosophy

The website behaves like an editorial portfolio with business-conversion paths embedded into it.

Do not begin with a list of deliverables.

Lead with point of view, work, imagery, and cultural relevance. Then make services and inquiry easy to understand.
