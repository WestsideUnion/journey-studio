# 03 — Homepage Specification

## Section 01 — Hero

Full viewport or near-full viewport.

### Layout

Desktop:
- navigation across top
- dominant photography background
- optional split / collage crop
- giant stacked `JOURNEY STUDIO`
- small descriptive copy lower left
- services / disciplines micro-list lower right
- CTA row
- subtle section index

Copy:

**JOURNEY  
STUDIO**

`CREATIVE · CONTENT · CONNECTION`

A creative studio for brands, people and communities who believe in meaningful stories.

CTA:
`Start a Project`

Secondary:
`View Work`

Categories:
Photography / Film / Creative Direction / Social / Community / Art

Use a dark cinematic image with blue practical light, blue window glow, city-night light, reflective glass, motion, or silhouette.

No generic stock office imagery.
