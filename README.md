# Journey Studio

**Creative · Content · Connection**

A creative studio for brands, people, and communities who believe in meaningful stories.

---

## About

Journey Studio is a multidisciplinary creative practice founded by Maheen, operating at the intersection of cinematic visual storytelling, culture, and community connection.

### Practice Areas

- **Photo + Film**: Editorial & commercial photography, documentary cinematography, and cinematic brand reels.
- **Creative Direction**: Visual treatments, campaign concepts, and holistic brand worlds.
- **Social + Content**: Culture-forward storytelling, UGC, and platform-native visual narratives.
- **Community & Events**: Curated cultural gatherings, salon series, and experiential activations.
- **Brand Strategy**: Narrative positioning and visual communication systems.
- **Art & Visual Worlds**: Limited-edition archival prints, digital works, and collector commissions.
